# Values of a Billing System

The Commerce Platform builds the Billing System. Billing aggregates usage events from products and converts them into invoices and credit card charges. Without Billing, the company collects no money. Without money, there is no company.

Through this work, we have come to value:
* Financial correctness over meeting a deadline
* Big-picture planning over focusing solely on my MVP
* Innovation and agility over always following the established path
* Proactive modernization over ignoring legacy software
* Sustainable development over maximum speed

That is, while there is value in the items on the right, we value the items on the left more.

---

## Financial correctness over meeting a deadline

Meeting deadlines is important. Others depend on us to make a good faith effort to fulfill our commitments. However, if the numbers don’t add up, nothing we build matters. The billing system is a financial system first and foremost.

_No matter what we promised, we can’t call the work done until we are confident that the math is correct._

## Big-picture planning over focusing solely on my MVP

Building a minimum viable product is a powerful tool for delivering customer value quickly and iterating from there, but an MVP is just the beginning. It’s the first step toward a feature-rich and lovable product, which requires having the end state in mind. While we may start by shipping an MVP, we design and plan with the big-picture vision. Furthermore, MVPs are often focused on one team building one use case, but the big-picture vision almost always involves the work of many teams.

_Without considering the entirety of the design, we can easily paint ourselves into corners that are costly to escape later._

## Innovation and agility over always following the established path

There is a long history of building software in a particular way. This approach works, and a lot of effort has gone into refining it. But the established path isn’t the only path. Sometimes it’s necessary to break with tradition and innovate. Old ways are not always best. While we don’t fear the road less traveled, innovation must also serve the customer’s needs.

_We are not afraid to break with tradition if doing so helps us build a better billing system._

## Proactive modernization over ignoring legacy software

Why fix what isn’t broken? Just put it in a box and close the lid. While tempting, old software eventually breaks, and without periodic attention, nobody remembers how to fix it. Instead, we proactively make time to keep our software up to date. We update our dependencies, patch security vulnerabilities, and refresh our tech stacks.

_We stay ahead of the curve because it's the responsible thing to do as engineers._

## Sustainable development over maximum speed

Delivery goals are important. They help focus our efforts and drive prioritization. That said, there’s a difference between a marathon and a death march. We strive to balance a brisk pace of feature development with investments in operational excellence to ensure our services are well designed, easy to operate, clearly documented, and easy to administer. A quiet on-call rotation lets us focus on building cathedrals instead of putting out fires.

_That which has been done well has been done quickly enough._

---

These are the principles that make Commerce unique, above and beyond our company values and the principles of Agile.

_Festina lente_: “Make haste, slowly”
